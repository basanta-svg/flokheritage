Every membership keeps a tradition alive, here's how you can be part of it.

Why It Matters

A living heritage, kept alive by its community.

Folk Heritage is more than a collection behind glass, it's a working craft village, a restored 18th-century manor, and a place where traditional skills are still taught, practised and passed on.

None of that continues on its own. Members, interns, volunteers, donors and partners each play a part in conserving the collection, supporting local artisans, and keeping the manor open to the next generation of visitors.
