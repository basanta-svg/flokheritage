One Month

Internship Program

A one-month internship with Folk Heritage includes:

  • One-year museum membership
  • Completion certificate
  • Three complimentary entries
  • Free program participation
  • Priority seasonal access
  • One complimentary learning session
  • 20% discount at the shop and Craft Village

72 Hours

Volunteerism

Volunteers who complete 72 hours of service with Folk Heritage receive:

  • Certificate of service
  • Two complimentary entries
  • Program & workshop access
  • Free selected program participation
  • Priority workshop registration

Donation

Donate a Collection

Supporters can fund the conservation of a specific object in the collection. In return, donors receive recognition through exhibition display, an appreciation certificate, and annual updates on how their contribution has been used.

Donation categories and their conservation costs will be listed here soon. Contact us for current options.

How It Works

The Three-Program Framework

Program Public Receives FHM Gains
Donate Clear purpose & evidence of use Conservation funding, education, artisan support
Membership Regular access, learning, participation Recurring income, committed community
Collaboration Defined projects, institutional engagement Expertise, sponsorship, promotion, technical support
