Inside the Manor

Ground Floor

See Medieval Bhutan

Built for the animals first.

The ground floor has three rooms. The front room stores equipment such as leather bags, cane and bamboo items, saddles, stirrups, and other animal-related items, especially for beasts of burden. The main room was reserved for pregnant and milking cows, as well as sick animals. Adjacent to it is the calves’ room (buchung-go ray), where calves are kept separately from their mothers at night so that they do not suckle all the milk.
