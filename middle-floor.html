Inside the Manor

Middle Floor

Middle Floor- Store Room (Barthog)

The Barthog has two rooms, which are used for storing agricultural products and other household articles that are not used daily. If the family is large, the rooms may also be used for sleeping and eating. The rooms on this floor are usually dark, making them ideal places for children to play hide-and-seek.

The first room is used for storing grains such as paddy, wheat, barley, and buckwheat. The harvested paddy is stored in a big wooden container called a Bham and in other large baskets called Luep.

The other room is used for storing the odds and ends of the house, such as clay pots, wine and spirit containers, a milk churner, a mortar and pestle for crushing chilies and other spices, carpentry equipment, and traditional sports items.

What's Here

Two rooms, a year's provisions.

Kept cool in the wind-sheltered rooms.
