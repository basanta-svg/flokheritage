General Information

Opening Hours

0900 to 1700 hours. Closed on government holidays, but open during Tsechus.

Tickets

Nu 300 per head for tourists. Nu 100 per head for Bhutanese nationals.

School Visits

Schools wishing to bring students to the Museum should inform us 2 days in advance. No fees will be charged.

Weddings in the Manor

For hosting a marriage within the Manor, please inform us at least 2 days in advance.
