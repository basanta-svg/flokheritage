Autumn Cham Evening

A private performance of masked cham dance in our courtyard, staged by dancers visiting from Punakha, on the evening's themes of protection and harvest.

An Evening of Masked Dance

Protection, harvest and the turning season.

As the harvest draws in and the evenings cool, Folk Heritage opens its courtyard for a rare, intimate cham performance. The dancers, visiting from Punakha, bring with them masks and costumes carried down through generations, each gesture and turn rooted in centuries of ritual meaning.

Cham is far more than spectacle. Performed traditionally at monastic festivals, it is understood to ward off harm and bless the community for the season ahead. This evening's staging keeps that spirit intact, set against the rammed-earth walls and timber eaves of the manor rather than a monastery courtyard, but carrying the same weight of intention.

Seating is limited to keep the courtyard intimate. Guests are welcome to arrive early to view the current Treasures collection before the performance begins.
