Entrance

First impressions, the stone-paved courtyard and timber facade that set the tone for everything inside.

Step Inside

A cultural enigma.

Showcasing the timeless skills of Bhutan's rural artisans, step into a realm where tradition and craftsmanship are revered and preserved. The stone-paved courtyard and timber facade set the tone for what lies inside, a working farmhouse turned living museum, restored rather than reconstructed.

Enter this cultural enigma, for an unforgettable experience, past a hearth of daily incense, a water-propelled stone mill, a water-driven prayer wheel, and the enigmatic Fortress of the Subterranean Beings.

What's Here

A working entrance, not a lobby.

  • A hearth of daily incense, lit each morning as it has been for generations
  • A water-propelled stone mill, once used to grind grain for the household
  • A water-driven prayer wheel, turning continuously with the flow of the stream
  • The Fortress of the Subterranean Beings, a small shrine to the spirits believed to dwell beneath the earth

First Impressions

Arriving as a guest once did.

Visitors today pass through the same courtyard a guest of the Kawang family would have crossed nearly three centuries ago, past the mill and prayer wheel, under the shingle roofline, and into a house built to be lived in rather than admired from a distance.
