Our Story

About

Phelchey Toenkhyim — a living record of Bhutan's rural past, founded in 2001 under the patronage of Her Majesty the Queen Mother.

Governance

Advisory Committee

The Advisory Committee provides strategic advice and guidance in executing the Board's decisions — advising management on heritage preservation programs and financial sustainability, helping develop resource mobilization strategies, and contributing feedback on the museum's programs and initiatives.

  • Mr. Kunzang Thinley (Chairman) — Former Principal, Royal Academy for Performing Arts
  • Mr. Singye Samdrup — Specialist, National Library and Archive
  • Mr. Yeshi Samdrup — Chief Architect, Department of Culture and Dzongkha Development
  • Mr. Tshering Tashi — Proprietor, Jojo's Adventure & Founding Member, Tarayana Dragons Club
  • Mr. Kinley Tshering — Associate Lecturer, Royal College of Thimphu
  • Ms. Tshewang Palden — Principal, National Institute of Zorig Chusum, Thimphu
  • Ms. Sonam Pem — Executive Director, Tarayana Foundation
