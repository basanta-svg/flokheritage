Folk Heritage Specials

Signature Experiences

Beyond experiencing medieval Bhutan, the Museum is an ideal place for several signature experiences.

Living Heritage

Folk Heritage Craft Village

The Folk Heritage Craft Village was established to commemorate the 70th Birth Anniversary of His Majesty the Fourth Druk Gyalpo, Jigme Singye Wangchuck. It reflects His Majesty's enduring commitment to preserving Bhutan's cultural heritage through the continuity of traditional knowledge and craftsmanship.

Located within the Folk Heritage Museum, the Craft Village provides local artisans with a dedicated space to create, demonstrate, and market traditional crafts while offering visitors an authentic experience of Bhutan's living heritage. Visitors can engage with skilled artisans, learn about traditional techniques, and appreciate the cultural significance of Bhutan's diverse craft traditions.

Built using traditional rammed-earth architecture, the Craft Village is a living cultural space that supports artisan livelihoods, strengthens the transmission of traditional knowledge, and contributes to the preservation of Bhutan's rich cultural heritage.

The Craft Village offers a diverse collection of authentic, locally made products, including masks, traditional paintings, jewelry, garments, and other handcrafted creations by Bhutanese artisans — each piece a meaningful souvenir, reflecting the richness of Bhutanese culture and the skill of its traditional craftsmanship.

Just outside the museum lies an open space inviting you to experience Bhutan's national sport — archery. This area is specially designated for visitors to try their hand at this traditional pastime, which holds a significant place in Bhutanese culture. With expert guidance available, you can learn the techniques and skills that make archery a beloved activity in Bhutan. Whether you are a seasoned archer or a curious beginner, this opportunity allows you to immerse yourself in a vital aspect of Bhutanese heritage, all while enjoying the beautiful surroundings of the museum.

The museum is an ideal destination for students and scholars to study Bhutan's rural way of life and traditional culture. Visitors can explore how people lived in the past, including changes in daily work, food, clothing and traditions, and the museum also showcases folk games and traditional activities. It serves as a field-based learning site where visitors can observe and study Bhutanese heritage and its relevance to present-day cultural evolution.

Though located in the heart of Thimphu, the museum offers a peaceful setting where visitors can slow down, meditate, spend quality time with loved ones, and experience a quieter side of the city. The traditional setting and surroundings provide an inviting space for reflection, relaxation and meaningful time together. The Folk Heritage Restaurant complements the experience by offering guests authentic Bhutanese cuisine and traditional dishes in a setting connected to the country's culinary heritage.

In the museum courtyard, visitors can observe women involved in traditional Bhutanese weaving using techniques passed down through generations — an opportunity to learn about the skill, patience and cultural significance behind Bhutan's woven textiles. Interested visitors are welcome to try weaving under guidance and gain a hands-on appreciation of this important living tradition.

The Folk Heritage Museum is a place where you have the opportunity to wear and feel the comforts of adorning traditional Bhutanese dresses.

Marriage is a sacred and special bond. Feel the experience of marrying in the traditional Bhutanese way, inside the 17th century mansion. You can leave everything to us. Just inform us a day in advance.

The Folk Heritage Museum offers live demonstrations of traditional Ara brewing, a long-standing Bhutanese practice using locally sourced grains such as wheat and maize. Visitors can learn about the traditional brewing process and, where available, participate in the experience and taste the freshly prepared Ara. The museum also demonstrates the preparation of roasted rice, a traditional Bhutanese food commonly enjoyed with butter tea.

Our experienced astrologer can tell you about your past, present and future through a traditional reading. Please book at least 3 days in advance.

Cleanse your past, clear your present and brighten your future by engaging in this ceremony. As you engage in this ritual, focus on removing the impurities in the mindstream.

The museum's cultural team welcomes visitors with traditional Bhutanese songs and dance performances. Visitors are also invited to join the performance and learn traditional Bhutanese movements and dance steps under the team's guidance.

The museum showcases a curated collection of garments, textiles, jewellery, paintings, woodcrafts and other products made by Bhutanese artisans. Each piece reflects traditional skills, materials and design practices that continue to form part of Bhutan's cultural heritage. These locally crafted products are available at the Folk Heritage Craft Village and the Museum Souvenir Shop — every purchase provides meaningful support to Bhutanese artisans and the continuation of traditional craftsmanship.
