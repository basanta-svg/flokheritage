Folk Heritage

Step into a living piece of Bhutan's history

Centuries-old walls, hand-carved timber and quiet ritual spaces still hold the rhythms of everyday life in old Bhutan.

A Message from the Founder

“

Message from the Honorable Founding Patron

Bhutan is blessed with a rich cultural and spiritual heritage that forms the foundation of our national identity. As our society encounters rapid change in the modern world, it is our collective responsibility to safeguard the traditions, knowledge, skills, and values that define who we are.

When the Folk Heritage Museum was established in 1997, I wondered whether the time had come for such an institution. The years since have affirmed that it was a timely and farsighted initiative. The Museum has become an important place where our people, particularly younger generations, can learn about Bhutan's traditional way of life and understand the significance of our cultural heritage.

I believe that heritage must not simply be preserved as objects within a museum; the knowledge, skills, and craftsmanship behind them must also remain alive. Our traditional practices must therefore be renewed, practiced, and passed on to future generations.

The Folk Heritage Museum is a pioneering effort in this endeavor. I hope it will continue to inspire pride in our heritage and encourage each of us to become responsible custodians of Bhutan's living traditions, ensuring that they remain vibrant and meaningful for generations to come.

Her Majesty Queen Mother Dorji Wangmo Wangchuck
Royal Patron, Folk Heritage Museum
