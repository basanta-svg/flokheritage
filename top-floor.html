The Family Floor

Where the household lived.

The top floor, with its three rooms, was the heart of the home, a lively hub with the family kitchen, a welcoming living room, and a revered altar. Lit by trefoil windows and warmed by the hearth, it was where the household gathered, ate and prayed together.

The thabtshang kitchen, the Baryap living area hung with swords and scarves, and the Choesham altar room, the spiritual heart of the household, home to the Drum, Cymbals, Bell and Conch Shell.

What's Here

Three rooms, the family's daily life.

  • The thabtshang kitchen, warmed by the household hearth
  • The Baryap living area, hung with swords and ceremonial scarves
  • The Choesham altar room, home to the Drum, Cymbals, Bell and Conch Shell

Built for the Weather

Lit by trefoil windows.

Carved trefoil windows and sliding wooden shutters let daylight into the kitchen and living area while keeping out Thimphu's winter cold, the same joinery and craftsmanship found throughout the manor's timber-framed upper storey.
