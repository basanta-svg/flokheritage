Workshop · 1 October 2026

Weaving Demonstration

A visiting textile artisan from Lhuentse demonstrates backstrap-loom weaving in the collection room, with pieces available to view up close between sittings.

Thread by Thread

The backstrap loom, worked by hand.

For this demonstration, Folk Heritage welcomes a weaver from Lhuentse, home to some of Bhutan's most intricate textile traditions, to set up a backstrap loom in the collection room. The loom is tensioned against the weaver's own body, a method that has shaped Bhutanese textile work for generations and gives each weaver direct, physical control over the tightness and pattern of the cloth.

Visitors can watch the loom being warped and worked at close range, and ask questions about the dyes, motifs and time involved in a single length of cloth. Finished and in-progress pieces will be laid out alongside the loom, giving a rare look at the stages a textile passes through before it is ready to wear.

The demonstration runs across the day in sittings, so visitors are welcome to drop by at any point and stay as long as they like.
